Questions

Who is actually carrying my parcel?

Another student or resident with a verified registration number, on the same campus as you. Handover is confirmed by a 6-digit code that only you receive, so a parcel can't be closed out as delivered unless it physically reached you.

What if two couriers accept at the same time?

Only one can. The claim is a single atomic write conditioned on the order still being unassigned, so the first request to land wins and the second is told the job is taken. Nobody walks to a pickup that isn't theirs.

What does it cost?

The fare is quoted before you place the order and scales with package size and distance. On a typical main-gate-to-block run it's about the price of a chai, and it goes to the courier.

Can I be both a customer and a courier?

Yes, and most people are. It's one account with a role toggle in Profile. The pool of couriers is just the pool of users who happen to be free.

When can I use it?

We're in build. Join the waitlist and we'll tell you when your campus opens — we're starting with one and expanding once the courier density holds up.
